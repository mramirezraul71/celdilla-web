Privacy

Version 1.0 · November 12, 2026 · celdilla.com, by Celdilla.

In short

  • This website uses no cookies, no analytics, no advertising and no forms.
  • We do not ask you for any information to read it.
  • If you write to us, we use your email and what you tell us only to reply to you.

Who is responsible

Celdilla LLC was filed in North Carolina (USA) in October 2026 and takes effect on January 1, 2027. Until then, this website and the emails we receive are the responsibility of Raúl, founder of Celdilla, at raul@celdilla.com. From that date, Celdilla LLC will be responsible.

Who hosts the website

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc. According to the GitHub Pages documentation, GitHub logs and stores the IP address of every visit for security purposes. Celdilla does not receive those logs. GitHub’s privacy statement explains what GitHub does with them.

If you write to us

Celdilla’s email runs on Google Workspace, by Google. We use your address and what you tell us only to reply to you, and we do not share it with anyone for advertising. We keep the conversation for as long as we need it to help you and, if you bought something, for as long as the law requires us to keep the sale records. If you want us to delete it sooner, just ask.

Links to other websites

From here we link to our guides, to Carta del Amanecer and to the kit’s page on Gumroad. When you follow a link, that website sees your visit, like any other, and may know that you came from celdilla.com. Gumroad applies its own privacy policy. The privacy policy of the Carta del Amanecer app, in Spanish, is at carta.celdilla.com/privacidad.html.

Your rights

We handle your email on the basis of our legitimate interest in replying to you. Google and GitHub process data in the United States; both state that they comply with the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework. You can ask us for access to your data, to correct or delete it, or object to our handling it, by writing to raul@celdilla.com. You can also complain to your data protection authority: in Spain, the Agencia Española de Protección de Datos (AEPD); in Brazil, the Autoridade Nacional de Proteção de Dados (ANPD).

Changes

If this policy changes, we will say so here, with its new date.
