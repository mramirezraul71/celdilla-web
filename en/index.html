    We use artificial intelligence to research, write and code, and we review what we publish before it goes out.

Why “Celdilla”

In Spanish, a celdilla is one of the six-sided cells of a honeycomb. No cell makes the honeycomb on its own, but each one is well made and fits with the others. That is how we want to work: small, useful pieces that together hold up something bigger.
